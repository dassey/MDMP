The rules that reject a bad option — a mission statement missing one of the five Ws, a course of action that is not distinguishable from another, a PIR with no decision tied to it — are in harness/agent/engine.py.

You do not need to touch any of that to give useful feedback. Telling us "step 4 asks the wrong question" or "that is not what a PIR is" is the feedback that matters.

Every unit, place, and operation named here is notional. The options are a starting point for a staff officer, not a product — the staff owns the words. Doctrine drawn from FM 5-0, FM 6-0, ADP 5-0, FM 3-0, ATP 2-01.3, ATP 5-19, and the TC 7-100 series.
